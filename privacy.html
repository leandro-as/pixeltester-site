PixelTester privacy policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the PixelTester app for Android and iPhone handles your information. In short: PixelTester does not collect, send or share any data. Everything it does happens on your device, offline.

1. Data we collect

None. PixelTester has no sign-up, no sign-in and no account. The app sends nothing to our servers or to anyone else's: we have no server and the app does not use the internet. On Android, the app does not even request the internet permission.

2. Ads, analytics and tracking

PixelTester shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools. The app does not use the device advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Device information

To show the Device information screen and fill in the report, the app reads technical screen and system details on the device itself: model, system version, resolution, pixel density, refresh rate, current brightness, HDR and screen type. These details do not identify you, are read only when the app needs them and never leave the device unless you share a report yourself (section 5).

During the tests, the app raises the brightness and keeps the screen on; when you leave the test, brightness returns to your previous level. No permission is needed for this.

4. History and settings

Each completed check (date, device, the result of every test, the notes you type and the verification code) is saved only in the app's local storage, so you can compare before and after. Settings (screen type, haptics and whether the introduction was seen) also stay on the device.

  • To delete: open History and delete one check, or use Delete all.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the history and settings.
  • Depending on your device settings, the system's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data. That backup is controlled by you and the system vendor, not by PixelTester.

Avoid typing personal information in notes that you would not want in a shared report.

5. Shared report

The image or PDF report is created on the device. It leaves the device only if you tap Share image or Share PDF and pick a destination in the system share sheet (for example, a messaging or e-mail app). PixelTester does not receive a copy; from then on the policy of the app you chose applies.

The verification code is calculated on the device from the report content. It is not sent or recorded anywhere; it only shows that the report was not edited after it was created.

6. Network and links

PixelTester works fully offline and makes no network connections. When you tap Privacy policy or Support in Settings, the app opens that page in your device browser; from then on the policies of the browser and of the opened site apply. Support uses public GitHub issues: do not post personal data there.

7. App stores

The app is downloaded through Google Play or the App Store, which handle data under their own privacy policies. These stores may give the developer aggregated, anonymous statistics, such as install counts or crash reports you chose to share in your device settings. PixelTester itself does not send this information.

8. Sharing with third parties

Because we collect no data, we do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

9. Your rights

Because PixelTester handles no personal data outside your device, there is no data held by us to access, correct or delete: what exists is inside the app, and you can delete it at any time (section 4). If you still have a request under the LGPD (Brazil), the GDPR or another data protection law, use the contact below.

10. Children

PixelTester is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect data from any user of any age.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page with an updated effective date. If a future version of the app ever handles data differently, the policy will be updated before that version is released.

12. Contact

Questions, suggestions or requests about privacy can be sent through the issues of the public site repository: https://github.com/leandro-as/pixeltester-site/issues
